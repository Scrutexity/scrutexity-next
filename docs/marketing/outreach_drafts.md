# Outreach Drafts — GLP-1 Teardown Hook

**Strategic note:** Every template below references the published GLP-1 Weight Loss Claim Audit at
[scrutexity.com/insights/glp-1-claim-audit](https://www.scrutexity.com/insights/glp-1-claim-audit)
as public proof of our claim-governance expertise. We lead with the teardown itself — not a promise
about what we could do — so the recipient can verify competence before replying.

**CTA rule:** Never ask for a demo. Ask for a **watched read** — a 20-minute call where they react
to their own claim inventory live. The reaction is the data.

## Watched-Read Call Script

**Opening:**
```
I'm going to stay mostly quiet while you read. I'm not looking for agreement. I want to see
which parts feel real, which feel wrong, and whether any finding would change what you do.
```

**Question order:**

1. What is the first thing you notice?
2. Which finding feels most accurate?
3. Which finding feels wrong, overstated, or irrelevant?
4. Which claim would you actually fix?
5. What would stop you from fixing it?
6. Who on your team or client side would need to see this?
7. *(If reaction is positive)* Who else should see this?
8. What do you currently spend to avoid this kind of risk — lawyer, consultant, internal review, agency QA, nothing?
9. *(If they pull toward more work)* I can run the full version on [other site/location/client]. That is $99 per site. Want me to?
10. Do not ask for payment unless they pull toward more work.

**Strong signals:**
- They ask you to run another location or client
- They pull in a colleague
- They name a real warning, ad disapproval, client dispute, or compliance incident
- They ask what it costs before you ask
- They ask for the export, report, or rewrite
- They want to compare multiple locations

**Weak signals:**
- "Interesting."
- "This is cool."
- "I could see people using this."
- "Send it over and I'll take a look."

**Hard boundaries:**
- Do not call it legal review or compliance certification
- Do not lead with demand-at-risk dollars
- Do not promise recovered revenue
- Do not send a report before the watched read
- Do not use banned words: "great", "exciting", "however", "delusion"
- Do not use: "let's be honest", "the reality is"
- Never invent proof, citations, or testimonials
