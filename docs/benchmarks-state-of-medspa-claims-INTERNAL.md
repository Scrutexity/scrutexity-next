## Internal plan fragments preserved (strategy only)

- **Positioning:** "Benchmark Intelligence / No Hall of Shame" — anonymized, operator-grade
  intelligence; never name operators. Publish aggregate claim-risk benchmarks that create
  market pressure without making the company radioactive to the operators it serves.
- **Distribution machine:** Public teaser → agency edition (client-ready) → PE/insurer
  edition → monitoring conversion (private clinic snapshot + Guardian pilot per report).
- **30-day launch path:** start with 25–50 public websites, anonymized; publish the first
  Medical Aesthetics Claim Risk Snapshot with clear caveats, anonymized examples, and a
  direct intake path. Do not wait for perfect data scale.
- **Q3 2026 report structure:** top unsupported claim types; fastest-growing risky
  treatment categories; GLP-1/IV/peptide/hormone patterns; before-after/testimonial risk;
  AI answer visibility + citation gaps; demand-at-risk context with methodology and
  excluded variables; questions PE buyers and insurers should ask.

## Re-publish gate

Any future public benchmark must include:
1. Documented dataset (sample size, selection, dates, exclusions)
2. Methodology (classification rubric, review process, inter-rater handling)
3. No fabricated precision (percentages rounded to honest granularity)
4. No operator naming without consent
