# Obsidian Vault Templates

Copy these into your Scrutexity Obsidian vault for structured daily operations.
Templates go in `_templates/` or paste as needed.

---

## Templates

- `daily-sales-log.md` — End-of-day sales and outreach summary
- `prospect-note.md` — Individual prospect record
- `agency-reply-note.md` — Agency partner reply log
- `claim-gap-note.md` — Claim pattern observation
- `sprint-fulfillment-note.md` — Sprint delivery log
- `weekly-ceo-review.md` — Weekly strategy review
- `objection-pattern-note.md` — Objection tracking
