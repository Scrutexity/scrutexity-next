## 7. Methodology

Scrutexity evaluates public business claims by asking four questions:
1. What exactly is being claimed?
2. What public evidence supports it?
3. What evidence gap remains?
4. What is the safer framing?

Claims are classified as Verified, Weakly Supported, Unsupported, Overstated, or Insufficient Public Evidence. We do not call claims false, illegal, or fraudulent. This report is a marketing-risk review, not legal, clinical, or regulatory advice.
