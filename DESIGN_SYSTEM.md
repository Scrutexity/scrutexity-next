# Scrutexity — Construction 2026 Design System

This file is the canonical guide for the public Scrutexity site.

## North star

**Built Environment Intelligence.** Scrutexity helps construction leaders understand emerging technology, verify what is ready, and make higher-conviction deployment decisions.

The visual system is editorial, industrial, and precise: oversized condensed-feeling typography, exposed grids, hard rules, numbered modules, technical labels, and a restrained safety-orange signal color. The design may take cues from strong architecture and industrial studios, but must remain original to Scrutexity.

## Audience

- Owners and developers
- General and specialty contractors
- Construction-technology companies
- Investors and corporate strategy teams
- Public-sector and standards organizations

## Core services

1. Market intelligence
2. Technical diligence
3. Deployment strategy

Primary technology coverage includes construction robotics, smart-site systems, BIM-linked digital twins, embodied intelligence, policy, procurement, and incentives.

## Visual tokens

| Token           |     Value | Use                                   |
| --------------- | --------: | ------------------------------------- |
| `concrete`      | `#f1efe9` | Primary background                    |
| `graphite`      | `#171816` | Primary ink and dark sections         |
| `safety-orange` | `#ff5a1f` | CTAs, active signal, emphasis         |
| `steel-mist`    | `#d9ddd8` | Reports, dossiers, secondary surfaces |

Use square corners, one-pixel rules, high-contrast bands, large uppercase headlines, and small monospaced metadata. Avoid generic SaaS gradients, glass cards, neon, excessive pills, and decorative stock imagery.

## Typography and motion

- Display and body: Inter Tight
- Technical labels: JetBrains Mono
- Headlines: uppercase, tight tracking, compact leading
- Motion: subtle opacity, 1–2px lifts, and measured transitions only
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Evidence discipline

Clearly distinguish verified facts, inference, and recommendations. Link research claims to primary or authoritative sources when published. Do not present vendor claims as independently verified performance.

Required boundary: Scrutexity provides research and strategic intelligence, not engineering, legal, investment, procurement, or safety advice. Field performance requires independent verification. Never promise savings, deployment outcomes, or regulatory approval.

## Global language

Preferred: market intelligence, deployment evidence, technical diligence, field readiness, confidence level, source-linked, pilot design, procurement signal, policy catalyst.

Avoid: guaranteed ROI, autonomous when only assisted, production-ready without evidence, proven unless the evidence supports it, compliance certification, safety certification.
